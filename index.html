How It Works

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How can I report a lost item?

Click “Report Lost”, fill in the item details, location and your contact email, then submit. You get a unique Item ID.

How do I claim my belongings?

Find your item in Browse Items, open its details and press “Claim This Item”. Provide ownership details; our team verifies them before handing the item over.

Is registration necessary?

You can browse without an account. To track reports in “My Reports” and receive notifications, please sign up with your college email.

Can I upload photographs?

Yes. Photos are optional for lost items and recommended for found items (avoid showing sensitive numbers).

How can I contact someone who found my item?

Submit a claim request. Once approved, you will be told where to collect the item — details are never shown publicly.

🛡️ Safety Guidelines

🔒 Never share passwords or OTPs.
✅ Verify ownership before returning valuable items.
📍 Meet at a designated campus location (security office, library desk).
🙈 Avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly.
